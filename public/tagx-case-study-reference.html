CONTEXT

One marketplace. Two sides to serve.

Clients want a faster, more transparent way to hire. Creative professionals need visibility, genuine opportunities and an easier way to manage bookings.

Built for local and emerging talent.

The project focuses especially on Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities such as Dharwad, Hubli and Belagavi, where strong creative work often has limited visibility.

Three moves shaped the experience.

01

Make the portfolio central to discovery

Creator cards surface work immediately, while profiles progressively reveal project details, services, ratings, reviews and verification. This balances portfolio-led evaluation with manageable information density.

02

Introduce Creative Match

Manual category search still matters, but project-based matching can answer the more useful question: “Who is right for this particular project?”

03

Connect hiring with project management

A shared workspace carries the experience beyond booking and keeps the brief, references, requirements, deadline, payment, files and status in one place.

The real design challenge was everything around the search screen.

A successful marketplace must create value for both sides.

Clients need faster discovery, trustworthy comparison and simpler hiring. Creators need visibility, genuine clients, professional portfolios and better project management.

Next, I would test Creative Match, portfolio comparison, availability, booking and project management with a larger and more diverse group, then refine payments and dispute resolution using real behaviour.
